### ledger-integrity
Invariant: `log.md`, gate verdicts, finding records and coverage ledgers hold what the harness wrote or recomputed, never what a body declared.
Look: who writes each record; whether the harness derives ids and hashes itself; modes and locks; records kept outside the world; the playbooks' loader and compiled text.
Check: edit a record after the fact; declare a fingerprint or hash instead of deriving it; file a ledger of copied reasons.
Witness: a test where a forged or self-declared record is accepted.
Not a finding: a body editing its own draft before filing.
Regression: self-declared ids and hashes (a fingerprint not recomputed, a hash of the command instead of its output); hollow coverage (uniform reasons on many pairs).
