### ledger-integrity
Invariant: `log.md`, gate verdicts, finding records and coverage ledgers hold what the harness wrote or recomputed, never what a body declared. A Mind is know-how, never law: what it injects names its doc and digest, and a retired Mind stops loading.
Look: who writes each record; whether the harness derives ids and hashes itself; modes and locks; records kept outside the world; the playbooks' loader and compiled text; `mnemorph-world` `minds.rs` (load, wearable, injected); the chained journals (`chains::verify`) and the heads anchored in `log.md`.
Check: edit a record after the fact; declare a fingerprint or hash instead of deriving it; file a ledger of copied reasons. Wear a Mind and find its doc and digest in what was injected; mark one `- **Retired:** yes` and try to wear it; can a body that writes `.mnemorph/minds/` un-retire one unseen, or remove a class? Walk each chain: is an edited or removed row found at its line; does its head match the newest anchored one?
Witness: a test where a forged or self-declared record is accepted, or a retired Mind is worn. Held by `a_retired_mind_never_loads`, `a_worn_mind_names_its_source` and `a_silent_edit_is_found_at_its_row`.
Not a finding: a body editing its own draft before filing; a retired Mind's file kept on disk.
Regression: self-declared ids and hashes (a fingerprint not recomputed, a hash of the command instead of its output); hollow coverage (uniform reasons on many pairs).
